# Tournament Calendar V2 — User Guide

## Find tournaments (`/tournaments`)

- **List and calendar views** with month navigation; calendar groups events by their **local timezone day**.
- **Filters:** free-text search, scope, organisations (multi-select with search), division scheme + levels, location, temporal status (upcoming/ongoing/completed), publication state. Active filters show as chips with organisation **names**; clear all or reset individually.
- Cancelled events are labelled "Cancelled" in the calendar.

## Tournament detail

- Name, organisation, location, dates/times in the tournament's timezone, description, website, divisions (e.g. "Men A"), watch links.
- **Participate:** if signed in, join with a role (player, coach, referee, organiser); withdraw the same way. Your role shows on the page.

## My Tournaments (`/my-tournaments`)

Everything you participate in, grouped into Upcoming and Past.

## Accounts without profiles

Participation attaches to your account; ask an organisation admin if your profile is missing from an organisation's roster.
